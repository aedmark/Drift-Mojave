A GPU-native video editor with native VEGAS Pro (.veg) interchange.

Drift Mojave is an open-source fork of Drift built for editors who want the tactile speed, drag-to-crossfade muscle memory, and binary .veg compatibility of classic Sony / MAGIX VEGAS Pro on Linux, Windows, and macOS.

No subscriptions, no accounts, no watermarks, and no cloud lock-in. Open your existing .veg timelines, convert Long-GOP camera footage to Apple ProRes 422 in-process, grade with live scopes, and export with zero-generation ProRes Smart Render.

Why Drift Mojave

Native .veg Parser

Reads VEGAS Pro 14–21 compound RIFF binary files directly—preserving RGBA overlays, track solo/mute compositing, and color.grade curve FX.

ProRes 422 & Broadcast MXF

FAQ & Honest Limits

What does the .veg importer translate vs. skip?

Drift Mojave parses tracks, clips, stream offsets, audio/video link groups, RTF titles, Levels, 3-Way Color Corrector, Vignette, Softlight Glow, Sharpen, Pan/Crop keyframes, Velocity Envelopes, Track Envelopes, Bezier Masks, Blend Modes, Markers, and Regions. Proprietary third-party Windows DirectShow/OFX plugins with no built-in Drift equivalent are skipped safely.

When does ProRes 422 Smart Render stream-copy apply?

Unmodified single-layer Apple ProRes 422 .mov segments matching the export resolution and frame rate are muxed directly via AVPacket pass-through. Segments with compositing, speed ramps, or color effects fall back cleanly to full re-encoding.

How is Drift Mojave related to CutWire Studios' Drift?

Drift Mojave is a GPL-3.0 fork of CutWire-Studios/Drift, contributing standalone interchange, ProRes transcode, and test fixes back upstream while shipping full VEGAS Pro parity releases for editors today.
